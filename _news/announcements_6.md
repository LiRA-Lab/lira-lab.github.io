LiRA Lab participated in the [7th Turkish Robotics Conference](https://research.ku.edu.tr/turkish-robotics-conference/) held at Koç University, Istanbul, on July 19–21, 2026. Ece Kunduracioglu and Huzaifa Salahuddin delivered workshop presentations on behalf of Asst. Prof. Özgür S. Öğüz on the first day, while Asst. Prof. Öğüz gave a talk on the final day. Several lab members also presented their research during the poster sessions.
